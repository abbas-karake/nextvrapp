## Project-created art

Roads, sidewalks, lane markings, streetlights, signs, collision geometry, skyline composition, and controller-reactive hands are original procedural geometry created for this project. They do not contain third-party textures or branding.

## Runtime selection and optimization

Only the selected GLB/FBX files used by the application are committed. Original ZIP archives, Blender sources, duplicate formats, and the temporary download environment stay under `.asset-sources/`, which is excluded from Git.
